### **Backstory**
This is an unnamed DTK-branded XT clone! There's not very much
information about it online, other than a couple of pictures of
similar models. When I found this particular unit, it had the keyboard
forcibly cut off - and of the very few reports of this machine that i
can find, it originally had Cherry switches on its keyboard, which
leads me to believe that it was used solely for harvesting its
keyboard.

I've dumped the BIOS and other information from this machine.
